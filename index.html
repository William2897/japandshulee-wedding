Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can we take photos and videos during the wedding?

A: We would love for you to be fully present with us during the ceremony. Afterwards, please feel free to take as many photos and videos as you like! We will have a QR code at each table for you to easily upload and share your favorite snapshots from the day with us.

Q: What about wedding gifts?

A: Your presence at our wedding is the only gift we ask for! However, should you wish to honour us with a gift, a contribution towards our future together (Ang Pao) would be warmly appreciated.

Q: Can I bring a guest or a +1?

A: We have a carefully selected guest list due to venue capacity. Your invitation is just for you unless a +1 has been specifically named. The RSVP form will allow you to confirm for the guests included in your invitation. We appreciate your understanding!

Q: Will there be parking at the venue?

A: Yes, there is ample parking space available at the venue for all our guests.
